# GKORAN STOILKOVITS
**Visual Artist**

## About

I am a visual artist developing independent digital infrastructure to support the documentation, presentation, organization, and long-term preservation of my artistic work.

My approach places artistic authorship, the integrity of original works, and documentary reliability at the center of digital development.

## Atelier Stoilkovits

**ATELIER STOILKOVITS** is the creative framework for my artistic practice, documentation, and related initiatives.

It brings together the creation of original artworks and the development of resources for their documentation and preservation.

## STOILKOVITS.ART

[STOILKOVITS.ART](https://stoilkovits.art) is being developed as a public catalogue and archive of my artistic work.

The initial website is designed to be simple, accessible, and independent of commercial storefront functionality.

## Digital Infrastructure

I am exploring and developing digital methods and systems for:

- Artwork identification and documentation
- Digital cataloguing and archival organization
- Provenance and preservation of source materials
- Sustainable, maintainable web publishing

These initiatives are works in progress, developed in response to practical artistic and archival needs.

---

**Artistic authorship remains the foundation. Technology serves the work, not the other way around.**
